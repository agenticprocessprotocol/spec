## Structural review items pending Successor Foundation discussion

The following structural questions concerning the Interim Custodian IP posture are flagged for review by the Successor Foundation (AAIF or equivalent) as part of the matriculation discussion. They do not affect the operability of the current instruments for Draft Specification consultation under CSL 1.0 §9.6.

### 1. Interim Custodian IP commitment scope

**Status:** Resolved by design, flagged for Successor Foundation confirmation.

Vach AI Limited acts as both Interim Custodian and a Contributor. Under CSL 1.0 §2.1, Vach's Necessary Claims reading on the Specification are licensed by virtue of Vach's Contributions, in the ordinary course. Vach has filed provisional patent applications; provisionals confer no enforceable rights until converted and granted. No separate IP declaration beyond Vach's standing as a Contributor is required for the current baseline. The Successor Foundation is invited to confirm this reading at matriculation.

### 2. Matriculation IP continuity

**Status:** Resolved by design (CSL 1.0 §9.13), flagged for Successor Foundation confirmation.

Contributor Necessary Claims committed under Contribution Terms v0.3 are non-retroactive per CSL 1.0 §9.13. On matriculation to the Successor Foundation, the Contribution Terms will be replaced by the Successor Foundation's equivalent instruments, but contributions made under v0.3 remain valid with rights transferring intact (Contribution Terms v0.3 §7). The Successor Foundation is invited to confirm compatibility with its own IP continuity framework.

### 3. Governing law alignment

**Status:** Flagged for Successor Foundation discussion.

Contribution Terms v0.3 §8 and WG Data Handling Charter §12 specify DIFC as the governing-law venue for the APP-specific wrapper terms. DIFC applies English common law and is procedurally aligned with United States and United Kingdom common-law jurisdictions typical of standards foundations. Alignment between DIFC and the Successor Foundation's own governing-law framework will be discussed at matriculation; adjustments, if any, apply only to future Contributions per CSL 1.0 §9.13.

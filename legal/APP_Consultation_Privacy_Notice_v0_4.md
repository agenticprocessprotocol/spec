## Privacy

**Version:** 0.4 Draft

This notice covers personal data we receive from anyone who comments on, contributes to, or gets in touch about the Agentic Process Protocol ("**APP**"). It is written to be read, not to be survived.

#### Who is responsible

**Vach AI Limited**, registered in the Dubai International Financial Centre, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (the "**Interim Custodian**"), hosts the APP consultation and is responsible for personal data received through this site. We hold this role on an interim basis pending transfer to the designated Successor Foundation.

APP is being developed under the stewardship of a Working Group of member organisations, with the intention of matriculating the work — and this site — to an independent standards foundation (the "**Successor Foundation**"). The specific Successor Foundation is a Working Group decision to be announced when made; no matriculation agreement is in place at the effective date of this notice. When the transfer happens, we will tell you before it does and update this notice.

**On matriculation:** this notice will be replaced by the Successor Foundation's equivalent notice; personal data you gave us continues to be handled at least to the standard set out here, with your rights not diminished by the transition.

The Working Group is the **intended beneficiary** of your submission. We hold your data in trust for that purpose.

#### Who else is responsible

For the parts of the consultation we run jointly with the Working Group — receiving your comment, sharing it with Members for review, publishing the disposition record — the Interim Custodian and each Working Group Member are **joint controllers** under Article 26 of the EU GDPR. The essence of our arrangement is in the box below. The full arrangement is in the **Working Group Data Handling Charter** at `/charter`.

> **Article 26 essence.**
> - You can exercise your data-protection rights against the Interim Custodian *or* against any Working Group Member. You do not need to work out which of us to write to.
> - **Central contact:** **privacy@agenticprocess.org**. We will coordinate with Members on your behalf.
> - **Transparency:** the Interim Custodian maintains this notice. Members honour it.
> - **Consent:** the Interim Custodian captures and manages your consent. Members are bound by it. If you withdraw consent, the Interim Custodian notifies Members and they must action the withdrawal within thirty (30) days.
> - **Requests:** normally answered within one month by whichever of us you write to.
> - **Breaches:** each of us handles regulator notification for our own systems. The Interim Custodian leads communication to you where the breach affects central infrastructure.
> - **Amendment:** we review the arrangement annually and republish the essence here when it changes.

For anything a Member does inside its own systems — its internal review workflow, its own retention, internal briefings — the Member is an independent controller and its own notice applies.

#### What we collect

**When you comment or write to us:**

- **Who you are and how to reach you** — your name and email address.
- **Where you're writing from and for** — your country or region, and (optionally) your organisation and role.
- **What you're telling us** — your comment, the section it refers to, and anything else you send.
- **How you'd like to be attributed** — your choice of public, pseudonymous, or anonymous submission.
- **What we write down** — our own notes if we talk with you.

**When you use the site:**

- **Basic technical information** — IP address, browser, pages visited, referrer. We use this to run the site, keep it available, and defend against abuse.
- **Analytics, if you allow them** — we ask before we set any non-essential cookies. Details in our cookies notice at `/cookies`.

If you submit anonymously, don't include your name or contact details in the comment body. We won't strip them out for you.

We do not ask for, and would rather you did not send: date of birth, national ID, passport numbers, health information, or anything about your religion, ethnicity, politics or sexual orientation. If you send this despite that request, we will not use it in the consultation. We will delete it or restrict access to it as soon as reasonably practicable, subject to applicable law, legal claims, and our backup and record-management processes.

**A person reads every comment.** We may use software, including AI assistants, to help organise, cluster, translate, and summarise submissions. Substantive analysis and any decision about your comment is done by Working Group Members. No decision about you, or your comment, is made solely by automated means. The AI tooling we use in central operations is listed at `/ai-tooling`.

#### What we use it for

We use your information to:

- read, consider, and respond to your comment as part of APP development;
- publish your comment as part of the public consultation record, according to the attribution you chose at submission;
- follow up with you if we need clarification;
- keep you informed about the protocol's progress if — and only if — you opt in;
- run and secure the consultation website;
- keep records of who participated, so we can acknowledge contributors in the published protocol (again, according to your attribution choice).

Your **substantive comment content** may be retained indefinitely as part of the public protocol record, even after your personal identifiers are pseudonymised or removed.

By submitting a comment, you also agree to the **APP Contribution Terms** at `/contribution-terms`, which adopt the Linux Foundation's **Community Specification License 1.0** verbatim and grant the Working Group and its Successor Foundation the licences needed to incorporate your comment into the protocol. Those terms cover the words of your comment; this notice covers your personal data.

#### Why we can use it

- **Your consent**, for submitting a comment, for the attribution choice you make, and for any ongoing communications you opt in to. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to **privacy@agenticprocess.org**; withdrawal does not affect anything we did lawfully before.
- **Our legitimate interests** in developing APP through open consultation, running a fair and orderly process, keeping records, and defending the consultation from misuse. We have balanced those interests against your rights and expectations.
- **Legal obligations**, where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Working Group Members rely on their own legal bases for anything they do outside the joint processing, which they set out in their own notices.

#### Who sees it and who handles it

**Us.** A small number of named people at the Interim Custodian who coordinate the consultation.

**Our service providers**, on our instructions and only for the purposes above:

- cloud productivity, email and file storage, where our records live;
- consultation platform and forum tooling, if we host discussions;
- analytics and site security providers;
- professional advisers, where a matter requires it.

They act for us; they do not decide how to use your data, and they do not use it for their own purposes.

**Working Group Members** — the organisations developing APP. They receive your comment (and your identity, unless you submit anonymously) to review it and contribute to the disposition. They act as joint controllers with us for that purpose, and as independent controllers for their own internal processing. All Members are bound by the **Working Group Data Handling Charter** at `/charter`. Under that Charter, Members must not:

- use your identity for sales, marketing, prospecting, or competitive intelligence;
- share your data outside their organisation;
- input your data into model-training pipelines or into AI systems that retain inputs to improve models;
- correlate your submission with their customer records.

The current list of Members is at `/members`.

**The Successor Foundation**, once designated, will inherit our role. See *Who is responsible* above.

**The public**, but only according to the attribution you chose at submission:

- **Public** — your name (and organisation, if you gave one) appears alongside your comment in the disposition table and acknowledgments;
- **Pseudonymous** — a reviewer identifier appears; your name and organisation do not;
- **Anonymous** — no identifier appears.

You can change your attribution choice while your comment is still within the retention window. Write to us and we will update the published record within a reasonable period.

**We may also disclose information** where the law requires us to, or where it is necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

**We do not sell your data. We do not use it to train any model. We do not share it with data brokers or job boards.** We require the same, contractually, from Members and service providers.

#### Where it goes

We are based in the **United Arab Emirates**. Your information may be accessed or processed in the UAE, in the European Union, in India, and in other countries where our service providers or Working Group Members operate.

For international transfers of personal data, we rely on:

- an adequacy decision, where one is in force;
- Standard Contractual Clauses (EU/UK) or an equivalent approved mechanism;
- another legally permitted safeguard.

You can ask us for information about the specific safeguards for your data at **privacy@agenticprocess.org**.

#### How long we keep it

- **Comment records with personal identifiers:** for the life of the APP version to which they relate, plus five (5) years. After that, we pseudonymise personal identifiers; the substantive comment stays as part of the protocol record.
- **Correspondence not tied to a comment:** twenty-four (24) months, then deleted, unless we need to keep limited information for a legal reason.
- **Working Group member records** (attendance, authored comments, votes): for the life of the protocol, as part of the public record.
- **Site logs:** ninety (90) days for operational logs; longer where retained for security investigation.

On transfer to the Successor Foundation, records pass under the same retention schedule unless the Successor Foundation notifies you of a change.

#### Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **show you** what we hold about you;
- **correct** anything wrong;
- **delete** it;
- **restrict or stop** using it for a particular purpose;
- **object** to processing based on our legitimate interests;
- **send you a copy** in a portable form;
- **withdraw consent**, where we relied on it.

Some rights have limits. If your comment has been published or incorporated into the protocol, we may not be able to remove it from the public record on freedom-of-expression and public-interest grounds (GDPR Art. 17(3)). We can still remove your name from the record and revert your comment to pseudonymous or anonymous form — write to us and we will.

Write to **privacy@agenticprocess.org**. We will not ask you to explain why. We may need to check you are who you say you are before we act, because handing your data to the wrong person would be the worse outcome.

We normally reply within one month. The law may allow more time for complex requests; if that happens, we will tell you why.

#### Children

The APP consultation is not directed at children. Please do not submit comments if you are under 18.

#### Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the **DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection** (as our home regulator). If you are in the EU or UK, you can complain to your national supervisory authority. You do not have to complain to us first.

#### Changes

If we change this notice, we will change the effective date and version above. If a change matters — a new category of recipient, a shorter retention period, a new purpose, a designated Successor Foundation, or a transfer of hosting — we will say what changed rather than expecting you to compare versions.
